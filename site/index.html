Evidence and review details

  1. Problem

    Issue #4699 requested the ETag from an S3 log write so callers could chain conditional writes.

  2. Implementation

    WriteEvalLogResult returns the S3 write ETag from write_eval_log, or None for non-S3 writes.

  3. Review & repairs

    Maintainer @ransomr requested changes. Repairs covered ETag capture, tracing, missing-file behavior, documentation, and the Trio S3 read path. Read the repair summary.

  4. Accepted outcome

    @ransomr approved the repaired PR, which was merged on 27 August 2026.

AI assistance: OpenAI Codex assisted implementation, review, and validation. The public PR says the final diff and results were reviewed before publication. Maintainer review comments also disclose AI generation with maintainer review before posting.

What this shows: one specific contribution and its repair cycle. It does not establish speed, productivity, or general capability. A measured case study is a future possibility, not a result reported here.
